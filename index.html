Model provenance: a decision, not a checkbox

The Centre treats licensing, copyright, data provenance, bias, and sustainability as central to its work. This isn't compliance — it's pedagogy. Two real Hugging Face model cards illustrate the difference:

✓ CLEAR-Global / Kanuri TTS
Licence Documented
Training data 10 hrs, source named
Consent Explicitly stated
Ethics Section included
Limitations Documented
CLEAR-Global/TWB-Voice-Kanuri-TTS-1.0
✗ SatiricalGuru / Trump voice clone
Licence MIT (code only)
Training data Not documented
Consent None — cloned voice
Ethics None
Limitations Not discussed
SatiricalGuru/beatrice-voice-models

Both are downloadable in thirty seconds. The difference is whether you can assess what you're using before you use it.

  • If the licence or training data is unclear, flag it to the academic — suggest a well-documented alternative
  • Make the decision visible to students: understanding where a model comes from is part of working with AI critically
  • Provenance extends to the platform too — the July 2026 Hugging Face breach by an autonomous AI agent shows supply-chain security matters

Before, during and after the workshop

Before
  • Brief the academic on changes and constraints.
  • Check accessibility needs: seating, screen magnification, input devices and familiarity with the lab interface.
  • Prepare a quick-reference handout, a pre-loaded workflow and a known-good checkpoint.
During
  • Begin with a guided walkthrough.
  • Pair experienced students with beginners.
  • Stay present and step in when confusion becomes frustration.
After
  • Record the environment, versions, model checksums and licensing decisions.
  • Gather feedback and update the runbook.
  • Build a library of tested workflows and guidance for independent practice.

Lower the threshold of entry without lowering the ceiling of what’s possible.

Revolutionary Drapery: a LoRA experiment

Adapting an existing model

SDXL adapted using Hugging Face PEFT (LoRA), trained on 20 public-domain images using an NVIDIA RTX 3090 rented through Vast.ai.

Small adapters are trained while the base model’s weights remain frozen.

  • Resolution: 1024 × 1024
  • LoRA rank: 8 · training steps: 500
  • Batch size: 1 · gradient accumulation: 4
  • FP16 · gradient checkpointing enabled

Columns: base → 100 → 300 → 500 steps. Rows: body, cloth and space. Each row uses the same prompt and seed across checkpoints.
